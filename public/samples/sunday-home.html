  • Vacuuming and mopping suitable floors

  • Bathroom and kitchen cleaning within the agreed scope

Ask before booking

Exterior windows, high access, pest removal, repairs and hazardous waste are outside this sample offer. Oven and appliance interiors need a separate scope.

Questions before you book?

The sample service area covers Quezon City and nearby Metro Manila neighborhoods. Your area is checked before a booking is confirmed.

Do I need to be home?

Access arrangements are agreed before the visit. Let the team know if you will be home or need another person to provide access.

What should I put away?

Clear loose clothing, dishes and personal items from the surfaces to be cleaned. Secure valuables and let the team know about fragile items.

Can I request a regular clean?

Yes. Choose Regular clean in the request and discuss a schedule when reviewing the estimate.

Does sending the form confirm a booking?

No. This portfolio form only previews a request. A real service would confirm availability, price and scope before booking.
