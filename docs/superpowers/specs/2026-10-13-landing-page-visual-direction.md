## Responsive direction

On narrow screens, keep the brand, headline, supporting copy and primary action in that reading order. Relay's workflow moves below the offer. Next Chapter's portrait becomes a separate crop below the introduction. Sunday Home uses a shorter image followed by the quote controls. Fieldwork shows product image, price, color and purchase action before the detailed features. Use compact section navigation and avoid overlapping sticky elements.

Dark variants use each brand's own dark ground and accessible accent colors. Photography keeps its natural colors. Product controls, forms and focus indicators require rendered verification during implementation.
